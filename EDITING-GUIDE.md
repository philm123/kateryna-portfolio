# Editing your site

Every word on your site lives in one content file. You edit it in Pages CMS, a free editor. When you press Save, your site updates in about a minute.

## Sign in

1. Open the invitation email from Pages CMS and follow its link to sign in. You don't need a GitHub account.
2. Open **kateryna-portfolio**. Check that the top left says **main**. That's your live site.
3. Click **Website content**. Each part of the page has its own panel, top to bottom.

## Change words

Edit the text in any panel, then press **Save** at the top right. Refresh your site after about a minute to see it.

- **Bold:** put two stars on each side, like `**$25M**`. That's how the numbers stand out.
- **Lists** (facts, results, credentials): **Add an item** adds a line. The trash icon removes one. Drag the dotted handle on the left to reorder.
- **Roles, gauges and capabilities** fold up to a short title. Click the arrow to open one.

## The flight path

**Flight path** has the five stops on the globe: Kyiv, Daytona Beach, Atlanta, Mexico City and the AI years. You can change every word at each stop. The cities on the globe and the route the plane flies are part of the design, so they stay put. Ask Phil if you want a stop moved.

## The gauges

Each gauge has the number shown in the dial and how full the dial is, from 0 to 100. A plain percentage like `30%` counts up when it scrolls into view.

## Replace your resume

Scroll to **Contact and resume**. Under **Resume PDF**, click the trash icon to remove the current file, upload the new PDF, then press **Save**.

Your current resume has both phone numbers on it. Bots collect phone numbers from public PDFs, so consider a copy with only your email.

## Being found

Your site starts hidden from search engines. When you're ready, open **Search and sharing**, switch off **Hide from search engines**, and press **Save**.

## Undo a mistake

- **Small slip:** put the old text back and press **Save**.
- **Need an earlier version:** the clock button next to **Save** shows past versions.
- **Something looks broken:** tell Phil. He can roll the whole site back to the last good version in one click.

## Good to know

- Every Save publishes. Make all your edits, then press Save once.
- The globe, the plane, the window shade and the layout aren't in the editor, so they can't break by accident.
- Spanish and Russian versions come once your English text is final.
